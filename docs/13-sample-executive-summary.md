**Overall recommendation: PROCEED TO CONTROLLED FEASIBILITY AND SEEK EXCLUSIVITY**

**Overall risk rating: AMBER** (no hard-stop categories identified; three gate issues
considered resolvable through standard investigation)

This 0.42 ha former agricultural yard lies within the defined settlement envelope for
Meppershall, outside the Green Belt, and appears capable of accommodating an indicative
6–10 dwellings (base case 8) subject to the further investigation summarised below. No
statutory ecological or heritage designation intersects the site, and no Flood Zone 3
mapping was identified within the boundary. These are desktop screening observations based
on data retrieved on 14 July 2026 and do not themselves confirm deliverability.

**Key opportunities**
- Brownfield redevelopment of a redundant agricultural yard within an existing settlement
  envelope, consistent with the Local Plan's settlement-focused growth strategy.
- Existing vehicular access point (field gate) available for potential upgrade, avoiding the
  need for a new frontage to be created.
- No statutory ecological or landscape designation constraint identified within the site or
  immediate buffer.

**Key constraints**
- Surface-water drainage route and infiltration suitability not yet confirmed — superficial
  geology suggests clay, which may limit infiltration options.
- Former yard use indicates a potential made-ground/contamination risk requiring a Phase 1
  desk study.
- Apparent visibility at the proposed access point may be constrained by roadside hedgerow;
  a visibility-splay survey is required before the access strategy can be relied upon.
- The adjoining road's adoption status has not been confirmed with the Highway Authority.

**Immediate actions**
1. Instruct a Phase 1 desk-study contamination assessment.
2. Instruct a topographical survey and infiltration testing to inform a preliminary drainage
   strategy.
3. Commission a visibility-splay survey at the proposed access and open a dialogue with the
   Highway Authority on adoption status.
4. Instruct solicitors to begin title review and confirm registered ownership.

**Recommended spend cap for next stage: £12,500**, covering the surveys and reports listed
above, in line with BASECORE's standard feasibility validation budget for sites of this scale.
